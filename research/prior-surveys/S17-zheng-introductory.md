## Scope and assessment

This introductory review is organized from biological background through neuron models, neural circuits, learning mechanisms, and network architectures. Its strongest contribution is a pedagogical comparison between biological intelligence, SNNs, and ANNs. It is not a deployment review. Hardware, toolchains, evidence classification, and complete routes to physical chips are outside its actual organization.
