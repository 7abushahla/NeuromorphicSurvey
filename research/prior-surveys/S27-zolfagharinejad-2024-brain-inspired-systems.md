## Scope and contribution

This systematic review covers brain-inspired hardware, including neuromorphic, in-memory, reservoir, and hyperdimensional computing. Its organizing taxonomy is computing framework, then hardware platforms, applications, and energy efficiency. The strongest contribution is its documented literature-selection procedure and normalization of reported OPS/W figures to support fairer energy-efficiency comparison. Deployment depth is hardware-landscape synthesis, not software-route execution.

Material omissions include SNN training and conversion detail, framework or compiler comparison, interchange formats, semantic export constraints, evidence classes separating physical measurement from proxy estimates, and a traceable network route.
