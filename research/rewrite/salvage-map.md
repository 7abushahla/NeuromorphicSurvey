This map treats the existing site and dossiers as research inputs. `Retain` preserves a
supported explanation after editorial normalization. `Rewrite` preserves the underlying
question but replaces the argument. `Move` changes the dependency location and normally
also requires rewriting. `Remove` excludes stale, duplicated, unsupported, or generated
material. No numerical count or deployment verdict is copied directly. Counts are
regenerated from canonical data.

## Global removals and regeneration rules

1. Regenerate every paper count, population count, survey count, route count, and evidence
   distribution. Do not preserve a number because it appeared in a dossier.
2. Remove all duplicated inline reference lists from section fragments. Canonical
   bibliography generation owns metadata and aliases.
3. Remove any dagger or access qualifier that conflicts with the completed full-text
   audit. Retrieval history remains in research notes, not in current coverage prose.
4. Remove unsupported absolutes such as `never`, `every`, `only`, or `no platform`
   unless a canonical claim states the searched population and falsifier.
5. Preserve a table or figure concept only when its revised placement answers a named
   explanatory question. Generated data own table values and measurements.
